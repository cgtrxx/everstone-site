# Third-party assets

## Fonts

DM Sans and Manrope were obtained from Google Fonts. The full SIL Open Font License texts, including copyright notices, are preserved in `licenses/DM-Sans-OFL.txt` and `licenses/Manrope-OFL.txt`. Font files are not modified.

## Photographs

The eight photographs were already referenced by the supplied prototype through images.unsplash.com. Local JPEG versions are included to avoid third-party browser requests. Original source URLs, exact download URLs and file hashes are recorded in `docs/asset-sources.json`.

They remain labeled as design inspiration rather than completed Everstone projects. The original prototype did not provide photographer attribution or per-photo rights documentation. Confirm the intended publishing rights and retain appropriate evidence before a public deployment; this repository does not independently grant rights to the photographs.

## Brand assets

The supplied Everstone logo remains unchanged. The decorative SVG watermark is a simple geometric interpretation of its architectural symbol. No third-party license is asserted for these brand assets.
